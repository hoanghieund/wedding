Layout & Composition

  • Fullscreen: min-h-screen với centered content
  • 3-layer parallax:
    • Background gradient (slowest scroll speed)
    • Particle canvas với champagne/gold dots (medium speed)
    • Text content (normal speed)
  • Vertical rhythm: Label → Names → Date → Countdown → Scroll indicator

Typography Hierarchy

  • Couple names: Great Vibes 96px (desktop) / 56px (mobile), champagne color với text-shadow glow
  • Date: Playfair Display 24px, cream color, letter-spacing wide
  • Countdown numbers: Playfair Display 64px, light weight, champagne
  • Labels: JetBrains Mono 11px, uppercase, rose gold, tracking-widest

Animation Sequence

  1. 0-0.5s: Background gradient fade in
  2. 0.5-1s: Particles fade in và bắt đầu float animation
  3. 1-1.5s: "Save the Date" label fade + slide up
  4. 1.5-2.5s: Couple names fade in với scale từ 0.95 → 1
  5. 2.5-3s: Date fade in
  6. 3-3.5s: Countdown timer stagger reveal (mỗi unit delay 100ms)
  7. 3.5-4s: Scroll indicator fade in

Interactive Elements

  • Parallax on scroll: Background và particles di chuyển chậm hơn content khi scroll
  • Countdown live update: Real-time countdown đến 25/12/2026 09:00 GMT+7
  • Scroll indicator: Pulse animation nhẹ để hint user scroll xuống
